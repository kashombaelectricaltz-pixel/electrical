⚡ ELECTRIC FENCE INSTALLATION

Electric Fence is a service of installing a modern electric fence around your home, farm, or factory. This fence gives a small electric shock when touched, thus deterring thieves or intruders.

📋 OUR ELECTRIC FENCE SERVICES

  • Pole installation - Poles to hold the fence wires
  • Electric wire installation - Wires around the perimeter
  • Energizer installation - Electric fence power supply
  • Alarm system installation - Alarm that sounds when fence is touched
  • Signboard installation - Warning signs for visitors
  • Connection cables - Connecting wires
  • Maintenance and inspection - Regular maintenance

🛡️ BENEFITS OF ELECTRIC FENCE

  • Prevents thieves and intruders from entering your property
  • Alarm triggers when fence is touched
  • Not dangerous to humans (only small shock)
  • Works day and night
  • Has backup battery when power fails
  • Increases value of your property
